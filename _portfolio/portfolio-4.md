Ignacio was acknowledged for insightful discussions in "Deciphering Undersegmented Ancient Scripts Using Phonetic Prior," a TACL 2021 paper by Jiaming Luo, Frederik Hartmann, Enrico Santus, Regina Barzilay, and Yuan Cao.

The work developed computational methods for deciphering undersegmented ancient scripts and evaluated the approach on languages including Iberian. The project was recognized with the UNESCO/Netexplo Award in 2021.
