This editorial commentary by Frank C. Schuller and Insoo Hyun argues for adaptive regulatory approaches that can support patient benefit while keeping pace with AI-driven diagnostics.

The piece is relevant to Ignacio's broader work at the intersection of clinical AI translation, policy engagement, responsible deployment, and the institutional pathways needed for health AI to move safely into practice.
