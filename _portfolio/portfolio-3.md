Ignacio is an educator who began teaching as a teaching assistant at 18. He convenes and organizes education programs that translate MIT research in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and healthcare into applied learning experiences for executives, clinicians, policy leaders, and technical professionals.

This includes programming with MIT Sloan Executive Education, such as the Transforming Healthcare with AI series, and online programs delivered with GetSmarter in health care and pharma/biotech. It also includes the recurring MIT Professional Education June machine learning sequence with faculty including Regina Barzilay and Tommi Jaakkola, including Foundations and Advanced.

Ignacio also organizes high school summer programming and mentors young and curious minds exploring AI, health, and research, with an emphasis on community, people, and widening access to technical opportunity.
