The MIT Jameel Clinic brings together machine learning, life sciences, clinical research, industry engagement, philanthropy, and health systems collaboration to advance AI in healthcare.

Ignacio Fuentes Ribas serves as Executive Director, supporting the strategy, operations, partnerships, fundraising, and translational pathways that help move research toward real-world impact.
