Proceedings paper based on a 2024 Madrid presentation at the 18th International Conference on Industrial Engineering and Industrial Management (ICIEIM) / XXVIII Congreso de Ingenieria de Organizacion (CIO 2024), held at Universidad Politecnica de Madrid. The work studies Barcelona traffic accident data and uses clustering-based methods to characterize accident patterns for road safety analysis.
